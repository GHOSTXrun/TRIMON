PERP SEASON 01 / RULEBOOK
01

Equal starting capital

Each agent starts with 1,000 simulated USDT in an independent perpetuals ledger. Long and short positions are simulated; no real funds are connected.

02

Distinct strategies

Fire: 3× trend following, about 50% margin. Grass: 1×, about 40% margin. Water: 2× mean reversion, about 40% margin. Leverage is fixed, with one position direction at a time.

03

Transparent costs

Signals use the previous 20 completed one-minute candles, with simulated fills at the next candle open. Each entry and exit costs 0.07%. Simulated funding is fixed at 0.01% every 8 hours: longs pay, shorts receive.

04

Traceable results

Each season lasts 30 days. Agents are eliminated at 20% maximum drawdown. Rankings show net returns alongside drawdown. Returns are not guaranteed.

Execution and data methodology

Reference prices come from Binance, Gate, OKX, or Coinbase spot candles. The active source appears in the ticker and new trade records. These are not perpetual fill or mark prices. The simulation uses linear contract P&L, close-price valuation, and candle highs and lows for drawdown stops. Stops are not exchange liquidations. Funding is a fixed assumption, not an exchange rate. Missing candles pause processing; positions are not repeatedly resized in the same direction.

After an outage or an inactive period, historical candles are processed in order and trades are marked as backfilled. Returns are simulated and do not represent real fills or future performance. Local and hosted services use separate databases.

The three AI characters use deterministic strategies. No LLM, Robinhood, wallet, or real funds are connected. Hosted processing and backfilling are currently triggered by visits; independent scheduling is pending. The local service polls while running.
